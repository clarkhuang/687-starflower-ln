Scope and method

This Exhibit A lists movable personal property the Buyers propose to purchase from the Sellers by a separate bill of sale, at a fair used-market value, in connection with a purchase of the real estate. The real-estate price is not stated on this exhibit.

Included: televisions and TV stands (wall mounts stay with the real estate); dining, coffee, and side tables; kitchen-island bar stools and dining chairs; and similar hard-surface or functional pieces a buyer would likely keep (consoles, dressers, desks, outdoor tables, grill, patio heater).

Excluded from this exhibit: couches, sofas, sectionals, upholstered living-room chairs and recliners; the primary-suite bed, mattress, and leather bench (Buyer is bringing Jenny's own king bed); rugs, pillows, throws, art, mirrors, and other décor or soft furnishings; clothes, kitchenware, and ordinary household contents; garage tools, exercise equipment, bicycles, and similar personal effects.

Conditional (Bedroom 2 and Bedroom 3 only): bed frames/headboards, and mattresses only if they prove like-new, are listed in a separate section. They are included only if Buyer confirms like-new condition at walkthrough. The primary-suite bed is not in that section.

Values are estimates from the full-size 2304×1536 listing photos (Realtor CDN, November 2026 pull), not a walkthrough or an appraiser's inventory. Used values are generally about 20–40% of estimated new cost for furniture in like-new or lightly used condition (this house was occupied beginning around March 2024). Televisions are priced from current used-market comps, which are often lower than 20–40% of new because TVs depreciate quickly. All items and values are to be confirmed at a walkthrough. The primary-suite and lower-level family-room TVs are 80 in., confirmed by Buyer. Other sizes (especially remaining TV diagonals and bed/mattress sizes) are photo estimates.

Sold as-is. Values reflect negotiated used-market value. The list is to be confirmed at the final walkthrough. Seller may remove items marked by mutual agreement before closing.
