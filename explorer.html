      Controls

      OrbitLeft-drag or one finger · right-drag / Shift-drag / two fingers to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom
      Fly (F)W A S D to move, Q / E down / up, Shift for speed, drag to look. On a phone, use the joystick and drag to look.
      Walk (G)Fly at eye height (5′6″) on the current floor or the ground
      ViewsNumber keys 1–9 jump to the saved views · Numpad 1 / 3 / 7 front, right, top · Home overview · . or Numpad . frames the selection
      Identify (I)Click anything to see what it is and its size · H hides it · Alt+H shows everything again
      Measure (M)Click two points; you get the straight-line distance, the level distance and the rise · Esc clears it
      SectionThe slider cuts the house at the basement, main floor or roof
      Tour (T)Flies over the lot, then follows the trail to the VASA network. Any key or click stops it.

      Accuracy

      Walls, doors and windows are traced from the listing's Cubicasa floor plan at its measured scale (24.53 px per foot; the garage is 39′6″ × 49′11″). Heights come from the plans: main ceiling 8′-1⅛″ with 12″ coffers, basement finished ceiling about 8′, garage walls 10′ with a 4/12 scissor vault to a 13′ flat center, and a stair of 15 risers at 7⅞″. Furniture marked “approx.” is a typical size, not measured. The house is placed on the map by matching its garage to the aerial photo, within about 3 ft. The lot line comes from county GIS, and it is not a survey. The backyard path to the two-track is inferred, since it's under tree cover.
